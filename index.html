How much does house cleaning cost in Calgary?

Recurring cleaning starts from $49/cleaner-hour. One-time and deep cleaning is $60/cleaner-hour with a minimum of $196 + GST. Carpet and upholstery cleaning starts from $180. Pricing is based on actual time worked.

How long will the cleaning take?

It depends on home size, condition and service type. A standard cleaning usually takes a few hours, while deep or move-in/move-out cleaning for larger homes may take a full day.

Do I need to be home during the cleaning?

No. Many clients leave access instructions or a key.

What is included in a standard cleaning?

Kitchens and bathrooms are cleaned and sanitized, surfaces, baseboards and fixtures are dusted, carpets and rugs are vacuumed, floors are mopped and trash is emptied.

Do you clean inside the fridge, oven, or closets?

Yes, as part of a deep cleaning or as an add-on request.

Can I choose the same cleaner every time?

Yes. For recurring cleanings we try to assign the same cleaner or team.

Do you bring your own supplies and equipment?

Yes. Our team arrives with professional products and tools, everything needed is included. Our products are eco-friendly and safe for pets and kids.

Are your cleaners insured?

Yes. We carry $2M liability insurance and WCB coverage, and our cleaners are employees, not independent contractors.
